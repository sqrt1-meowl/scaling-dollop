# Curriculum source notes

## Active planning source

`functional-core-submitted.md` is the active source for designing the new grammar-and-vocabulary learning path. It prioritizes foundational function words, high-frequency verbs, school and community language, survival vocabulary, and reusable conversational chunks for students with less than two years of English exposure.

The document is a curriculum proposal rather than a licensed word-list distribution. Before implementation, its entries should be deduplicated, checked against openly reusable corpus lists, and grouped into teachable lessons with grammar objectives and practical contexts.
